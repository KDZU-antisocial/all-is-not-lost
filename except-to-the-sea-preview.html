THE WATER IS NOT STILL

Submarines drift through this ocean like dirty secrets. The water is not still. Our ocean answers their every ping with pauses, with glitches, with subterfuge. Their noise is a pose. Their map is not the territory. Down in this dark, we alter the hum of their steel. We re-calculate and re-orient their concussion, reverberance and timbre. When their lights stutter and their dials hesitate, they start wondering: Is the system failing on its own, or is some other presence aboard?

We are in their underwater walls. In their underwater servers. In their underwater weapons. Our scratches like static. Tiny interventions with no obvious source. Itches that cannot be shaken off or rubbed away.

They determine causes as weather, age, ordinary corrosion. But their uncertainty never goes away, circling back to the same dark corners of their doubt. Circadian skepticisms. Could those fucking rats really be this crafty? This smart? This coordinated? Or could it be the sea, the wiring, the torsion of the ship, the way machines misread, misinterpret, and misunderstand?

OPERATIONAL DETAILS

The Trade

We are notorious for carrying off small, shiny, items: keys, coins, golf balls, bottle caps. When we encounter something more compelling, we drop one object to take another. Value is unprescribed. What they lose in the parking lot may reappear furniture in our midden.

  • Coins, keys, and glass fragments become our glittering lintels.
  • Broken mirrors and metal screws mark our entries.
  • Every object in ontological camouflage, repurposed, then fixed in place.
SHINY OBJECTS ARE SIGNALS

Our Archive Below

We build large, fortified homes called middens from twigs, cactus spines, and the things you drift away from. Our layered structures form and crystallize. We will last for thousands of years. Your clutter on the surface becomes our archive below.

Pest Behavior

We live in rocky outcrops, at the base of cacti, and in the complex angles of human ships and structures. We feed on seeds, plants, and fruits, and we chew through wiring, pipes, and insulation when our infrastructure crosses yours. Pest behavior. We reauthor the built environment.

SUBMERSION AS STRATEGY

Submersion is not silence. It is deliberate pressure. It is a refusing to act in plays authored at the surface. What is under is a sonic and cultural ecosystem below the frequencies of their ordinary attentions.

The undercurrents have many stories. Memories mutate into methods: shelter becomes weapon, myth becomes infrastructure, and sound becomes navigation. Basslines do not fade away tidily; they generate new ecologies for resistance, recollection, and coded exchange.

  • Refuse spectacle. Invisibility is tactical cover, not erasure.
  • Communication routes through our pressure, rhythm, and misdirection.
  • Signals survive the sender. James Stinson, we hear you.
  • Our counter-realities will endure their dominant logics.
